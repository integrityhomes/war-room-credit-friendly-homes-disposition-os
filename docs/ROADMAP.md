# Roadmap

1. Foundation, property intake, validation, channel registry, Marketplace Guard, and tests.
2. Supabase persistence and Google Sheets property import.
3. WordPress property landing pages, inventory portal, and city pages.
4. OpenAI campaign factory with structured outputs and a property-fact guard.
5. Blog bot producing three useful articles weekly with review and duplicate protection.
6. REI BlackBook email, SMS, referral, and reactivation workflows.
7. Assisted posting center for Marketplace, Facebook groups, Craigslist, and local classifieds.
8. Buyer intake, matching, qualification, Call Now queue, showings, and application follow-up.
9. Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Meta housing ads, and Google Search Ads.
10. Analytics, content optimization, sold-property shutdown, permissions, audit logs, and backups.
