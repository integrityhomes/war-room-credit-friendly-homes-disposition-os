# Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts

This channel batch creates separate, trackable short-form social packages for Instagram Reels & Posts, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts.

Each platform receives its own Dwelyx tracking medium while sharing the selected campaign and property ID. This lets the Results & Attribution Center compare downstream registrations, applications, showings, contracts, and filled homes by platform.

The package builder uses only saved property facts. It does not invent financing terms, approval claims, repairs, condition, room counts, or property features.

Long-form YouTube walkthrough rendering, AI narration, music, editing, and downloadable finished video files are intentionally deferred to the later video-engine build.
